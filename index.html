About Me

I am an Associate Software Engineer at Devsinc and a recent graduate from the Ghulam Ishaq Khan Institute (GIKI) with a Bachelor's in Artificial Intelligence.

My passion lies in bridging the gap between cutting-edge AI research and real-world applications. I've had the privilege of conducting research at institutions like the University of Europe for Applied Sciences (Germany) and the University of Northern British Columbia (Canada).

I specialize in building intelligent systems, from LLaMA-based chatbots for ADHD support to computer vision systems for the visually impaired.
